1

Log page

Every attempt, actually recorded.

Log a single project you keep coming back to, or a full day at the gym in one go. Real grading — V-scale with the Camp5 colour chart for boulders, US grades with French equivalents for routes — so the numbers mean something.

  • Backdate a session you forgot to log, or leave it on today
  • Mark exactly where your hands started and finished on the wall photo
  • Tag the technique — heel hook, crimp, dyno, drop knee, and more
2

Live Log

Climb first. Log while it happens.

Set up the problem, tap start, and Chalkline runs a clean live timer for the attempt — plus a separate rest timer between goes. Fall at the quarter, half, or three-quarter mark with one tap.

  • Tap the wall photo to pin exactly where each fall happened
  • Jot what to change before the next try, while it's fresh
  • Keep going across sessions on the same project
3

Finish the attempt

Rate it, note it, done.

Once you send it, tag the technique that got you through, rate how satisfied you are out of five stars, and leave a note for tomorrow-you. Set the whole entry to public or keep it private — your call, entry by entry.

  • Same technique tags as your full log, so nothing's disconnected
  • A satisfaction rating that's about the climb, not the crowd
  • Public or private, decided per entry
4

Sharing

Send it. Then actually show it off.

Turn any log into a card worth sending — an achievement badge, a stats summary, or the full uncropped wall photo with every start, end and fall point marked and your attempt count right on it.

  • Native share sheet — straight to WhatsApp, Messages, anywhere
  • Every share copies a link too, no extra step
  • A scannable QR "name tag" for your profile, works from any camera
5

Community feed

A feed for climbers, not for scrolling.

Following, Suggested, and Good Deals — one feed for the people you climb with, one for discovering more, and one just for gear. Attach a real log to any post so a sent climb comes with the receipts.

  • Photo carousels sized to your actual photo, never cropped
  • Tap an attached log to open its full detail
  • Gear for sale, with the same likes and comments as any post
6

Sessions

Nobody sends alone.

Plan a session at your gym, invite the people you actually climb with, and let them join straight from the card. Hosting a course instead? Set the capacity and let people fill the spots.

  • Join or leave with one tap — hosts cancel their own instead
  • Comment on the session to coordinate before anyone shows up
  • Tap any participant's avatar to see who's coming
7

Chat

Message anyone you climb with.

Direct messages between climbers, with sensible gating — you can always message first, but further messages wait for a reply or a mutual follow. Shared posts and profile links open right where they point.

  • Block or unblock right from the conversation
  • Send a post or log straight into a chat
  • Message anyone directly from their profile

What's underneath it all.

The parts that don't show up in a screenshot, but decide whether the app actually holds up.

Real accounts

Secure email and password login, a guided sign-up that captures your level and qualifications, and a two-step account deletion that actually removes everything.

Live, multi-user backend

Your data is shared with your friends in real time, not stuck on one phone. Private posts are enforced by the database, not just hidden by the interface.

Progress you can see

Badges for grade milestones, hours climbed and total sends, plus grade-distribution and six-month activity charts you can filter by discipline.
